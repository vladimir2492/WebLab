Dataset: titanic3, Titanic passenger survival data
Download: https://hbiostat.org/data/repo/titanic3.csv
Documentation: https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/PASWR/html/titanic3.html
Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Replaces the previous 891-record seaborn subset.
1309 passenger records, 14 columns; 500 survived and 809 did not survive.
Downloaded CSV preserved without modification, stored as titanic.csv.
This is the standard expanded passenger dataset, not a current definitive
historical manifest. It excludes crew and reflects research available on
1999-08-02. Some ages are estimates; other values are missing.
Thomas Cason of the University of Virginia compiled improvements using
Encyclopedia Titanica, according to the dataset documentation.

Fields:
pclass: ticket class (1, 2, or 3)
survived: 0 = did not survive; 1 = survived
name: passenger name
sex: recorded sex
age: age in years (may be estimated or missing)
sibsp: number of siblings/spouses aboard
parch: number of parents/children aboard
ticket: ticket number
fare: passenger fare
cabin: cabin designation
embarked: C = Cherbourg, Q = Queenstown, S = Southampton
boat: lifeboat
body: body identification number
home.dest: home/destination

Empty fields are missing values, not zero.
For prediction, survived is the target. Exclude boat and body from inputs:
these record information from after the disaster and can reveal the outcome.
